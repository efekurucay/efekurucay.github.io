July 2025 Newsletter: AI Innovations and Productivity Tools

2 August 2025 · writing · history

July 28, 2025

As July comes to a close, I wanted to share some exciting developments from the world of tech and AI, along with practical tools to streamline your workflows. Here are the resources that caught my eye this month and might inspire your next project:

Featured Useful Links and Tools

  • Doppl: An experimental app from Google Labs that allows you to virtually try on outfits. You can upload a full-body photo of yourself or use an AI model, and then see how different clothes from your camera roll or screenshots look on you. The app can even animate your new look.
  • Hashtags: #AI #FashionTech #VirtualTryOn #GoogleLabs
  • Link: Doppl
  • Awesome-Selfhosted: A comprehensive, well-organized list of free software you can host on your own servers. It offers open-source alternatives to popular SaaS products, giving you full control over your data for tasks like communication, file management, and media streaming.
  • Hashtags: #SelfHosting #OpenSource #Privacy #Developer
  • Link: Awesome-Selfhosted
  • Stacher: A modern and user-friendly desktop application that provides a graphical interface for the powerful command-line video downloader, yt-dlp. It allows you to easily download, trim, and even subscribe to YouTube channels for automatic downloads without needing technical knowledge.
  • Hashtags: #VideoDownloader #Productivity #FreeTool
  • Link: Stacher
  • Liquid Metal Logo Generator: A fun and interactive web tool that transforms any logo or shape you upload into an animated liquid metal effect. You can adjust settings like dispersion, speed, and liquify in real-time to create a unique visual.
  • Hashtags: #DesignTool #Animation #GenerativeArt
  • Link: Liquid Metal Logo
  • Code2Tutorial: An AI-powered tool that analyzes a GitHub repository’s codebase and automatically generates a beginner-friendly tutorial to explain how it works. It’s an excellent way to quickly understand the architecture and logic of a new or complex project.
  • Hashtags: #AI #DeveloperTool #GitHub #Documentation
  • Link: Code2Tutorial
  • AI Engineer Pack: A collection of special offers, free credits, and extended trials for popular AI tools and services like ElevenLabs, Notion, and Mistral AI. It’s designed to help developers start and scale their AI projects at a lower cost, though it’s worth noting that many coupons are currently claimed.
  • Hashtags: #AI #Developer #Startup #Credits
  • Link: AI Engineer Pack
  • Cursor.Directory: A community-driven platform for users of the Cursor AI code editor. It provides a space to find and share custom rules (.cursorrules) to tailor the AI’s behavior for specific projects, discover useful integrations, and even find job opportunities.
  • Hashtags: #CursorAI #DeveloperCommunity #CodingTools
  • Link: Cursor.Directory
  • TalkyWork: A social platform designed for young entrepreneurs and professionals to build business connections. While it aims to foster a trade-focused community with features like Reels and events, potential users should be aware that some security analyses have flagged the site with a low trust score.
  • Hashtags: #Networking #Business #Entrepreneurship
  • Link: TalkyWork
  • Hugo Blox Blog Tutorial: The official guide for creating and managing a blog using the Hugo Blox Builder. It provides step-by-step instructions on how to create posts, customize the layout, and configure features, making it a great resource for academics, researchers, and professionals.
  • Hashtags: #Hugo #WebDev #Blogging #StaticSiteGenerator
  • Link: Hugo Blox Blog Tutorial
  • Google Fonts: A massive and free library of over 1,500 open-source font families provided by Google. It’s an essential resource for web developers and designers looking to easily integrate high-quality, fast-loading typography into their projects.
  • Hashtags: #WebDesign #Fonts #Typography #Freebies
  • Link: Google Fonts
  • MMAudio: An advanced AI model that synthesizes high-quality, context-aware audio directly from video content. It can generate environmental sounds, action effects, and ambient audio that matches the on-screen events, effectively adding a soundscape to silent videos.
  • Hashtags: #AI #VideoToAudio #MachineLearning #SoundDesign
  • Link: MMAudio on Replicate
  • InVideo AI: An AI-powered video creation platform that generates professional-looking videos from simple text prompts. You describe the video you want, and the AI handles the script, stock footage, voiceover, and subtitles, making it ideal for creating marketing and social media content quickly.
  • Hashtags: #AIVideo #VideoEditing #Marketing #ContentCreation
  • Link: InVideo AI
  • Musicca: A free online platform for learning music theory and skills through interactive exercises, lessons, and tools. It includes a virtual piano, a tuner, a metronome, and exercises for learning notes, chords, and scales, making it suitable for all levels.
  • Hashtags: #MusicTheory #Education #Freebies #Music
  • Link: Musicca
  • The Art of Manliness: A popular online magazine dedicated to helping men improve their lives through articles on personal development, practical skills, health, and style. It blends traditional values with modern advice to provide a comprehensive guide for personal growth.
  • Hashtags: #PersonalDevelopment #Lifestyle #Skills
  • Link: The Art of Manliness
  • Inception Labs AI Chat: This is a free playground for Inception Labs’ new Mercury Coder, the first commercial diffusion-based large language model (dLLM). Unlike traditional models that generate text word-by-word, it uses a diffusion process — creating a full draft and then refining it — to deliver responses up to 10 times faster, making it excellent for coding and quick Q&A.
  • Hashtags: #AI #LLM #DeveloperTool #DiffusionModel
  • Link: Inception Labs AI Chat
  • Kiro: An AI-powered IDE from Amazon (AWS) that uses “spec-driven development.” It aims to bridge the gap between instant coding and production-ready code by transforming developer commands into structured plans like requirements, design documents, and task lists.
  • Hashtags: #AI #DeveloperTool #IDE #Amazon
  • Link: Kiro
  • Wan AI: Developed by Alibaba Cloud, Wan AI is an open-source model capable of generating videos from text, videos from images, and images from text. It’s available for free trial on its website and can be downloaded for local use via ComfyUI (14B model is 31 GB, 5B model is 16 GB).
  • Hashtags: #AI #VideoGeneration #ImageGeneration #OpenSource
  • Link: Wan AI
  • Claude Mobile MCP Support: Claude Pro or Team plan users on iOS and Android can now access Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers on the go. This new feature expands Claude’s capabilities, allowing developers to leverage MCP tools directly from their mobile devices.
  • Hashtags: #AI #Claude #Mobile #DeveloperTools
  • Link: Claude App
  • BuiltWith: A very practical tool that reveals the underlying technologies of any website in seconds. Simply enter a domain name, and it will show you details like whether it uses WordPress (and its plugins), meta tag codes, server structure, and underlying software. It’s an invaluable resource for competitive analysis, developers, and anyone curious about website infrastructure. The free version is sufficient for most uses, with paid plans available for advanced details.
  • Hashtags: #WebTools #WebsiteAnalysis #DeveloperTools #CompetitiveAnalysis
  • Link: BuiltWith
  • GLM-4.5: GLM-4.5 is a new large language model from Zhipu AI that excels in reasoning, coding, and agentic tasks. It features a hybrid reasoning approach with a “thinking mode” for complex problems and a “non-thinking mode” for instant responses. The model has 355 billion total parameters and a context length of 128k, making it a powerful tool for developers and researchers.
  • Hashtags: #AI #LLM #Coding #AgenticAI
  • Link: Z.ai
  • Awesome AI System Prompts: This curated GitHub repository is a collection of system prompts for top AI tools, making it an excellent resource for AI agent builders and prompt engineers. It includes prompts for models like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and others, offering insights into how agentic AI systems are structured. The repository also provides a guide on crafting effective prompts, covering core principles such as clear role definition, structured instructions, and explicit tool integration.
  • Hashtags: #AI #PromptEngineering #DeveloperTools #GitHub
  • Link: Awesome AI System Prompts

I hope you found these resources useful. Until next month for more tech-filled updates!
