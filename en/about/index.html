About

I am 22, live in Antalya, Turkey, and study computer science at Akdeniz University. Part of the day goes to travel tech; the rest goes to turning things that annoy me into programs. Some get abandoned, some turn into products.

Ventures

My own work

Most of what I build on the side comes down to one sentence: turning a daily annoyance into a program. I got tired of hunting for shell aliases, so tam-cli happened. I did not want a second subscription for a coding assistant, so plexcode happened. Which also means I abandon as much as I finish — both live in this archive.

What I work on

  • On-device AI — systems that run locally and never call a server
  • Developer tools — small CLI programs that each do one thing
  • Turkish language models — where Turkish falls short, and fine-tuning it
  • Open educational resources — opening up university course material

Academic

I study computer science at Akdeniz University. I represented the department at the 20th BİLMÖK and was vice-president of the organizing committee of the 21st, spent a year as a Huawei Student Developers campus ambassador, and presented at the International Conference on CSE.

Communities

I started and run AkdenizOS: an open source archive of course material for computer science students at Akdeniz University. It holds over 100 repositories today — from data structures to microcontroller programming, discrete mathematics to database systems. Every term the same assignments get solved again and nobody keeps the solutions, so they would stop getting lost.
