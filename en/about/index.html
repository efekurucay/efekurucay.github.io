About

I live in Antalya, Turkey, and study computer science at Akdeniz University.

Lycian

My main work is Lycian. Two products are live.

FitBrand is a white-label platform for personal trainers. A trainer signs up, uploads a logo, picks a colour, and a client app in their own brand goes live in minutes. No app store review, no code — it installs as a PWA in one tap. Nowhere the client looks does our name appear.

tiro is closed-circuit AI for criminal defence lawyers. Neither the case file nor the draft defence leaves the machine; the local model we trained on Turkish criminal case files never connects to the internet. Every sentence it produces shows which page of which document it came from. It is named after Cicero's assistant Tiro: it records, summarises and organises — the lawyer forms the judgement.

Both point at the same thing: software that works without the data leaving its owner. The trainer keeps their brand; the lawyer keeps their file.

AkdenizOS

I started and run AkdenizOS: an open source archive of course material for computer science students at Akdeniz University. It holds over 100 repositories today — from data structures to microcontroller programming, discrete mathematics to database systems.

The reason is simple. Every term the same assignments get solved again and nobody keeps the solutions. So they would stop getting lost.
