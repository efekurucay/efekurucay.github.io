## About me

I live in Antalya and study computer engineering at Akdeniz University. I develop software for the travel industry. In my own projects, I build developer tools and work with on-device AI and Turkish-language models.

My graduation project, GiraffeGraph, is an open-source toolkit we’re building for creating and querying knowledge graphs. I also founded and run AkdenizOS, a community for people building products and starting businesses in Antalya. My About page has more on my academic work and communities.

## Ventures

Lycian is the software studio I co-founded in Antalya. We build two products through the studio, both covered in more detail on the Ventures page.

  • -FitBrand — A platform for personal trainers to launch an app under their own brand. Trainers can create AI-assisted workout plans, manage clients, and track their progress.
  • -Tiro — An offline desktop assistant for criminal defense lawyers. It reads UDF files, PDFs, and scanned documents, builds case timelines, and drafts defense submissions with references to the source pages.
