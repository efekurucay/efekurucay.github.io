attentube

March 2026 · project · JavaScript · source · history

YouTube, stripped down to its core: search and watch. No recommendations, no comments, no sidebar — just the video.

Why

You open YouTube to watch one thing and leave an hour later. The addictive part is not the video, it is everything around it. attentube removes the everything.

What it does

  • Search and watch, nothing else
  • No recommendation feed, no comments, no sidebar
  • Uses the YouTube Data API; runs in the browser
