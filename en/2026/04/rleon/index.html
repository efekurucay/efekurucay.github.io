RLeon

5 April 2026 · project · Swift · source · history

A macOS app that keeps everything on the machine: speech recognition, reading text off the screen with Vision, chatting with a local model through Ollama, and letting that model call system tools.

Why

Every voice assistant ships what you say to a server. So does every tool that reads your screen. When local models can do the same job, there is no reason for your microphone and your screen to leave the machine.

What is in it

  • On-device speech recognition — no network
  • Text extraction from the screen via Vision OCR
  • Local chat through Ollama
  • Native tool calling: the model can trigger system actions

Notes

Hardware sets the ceiling. Models around 7B are reasonable on a laptop; larger means heat and battery. Quality sits below cloud models — in exchange, nothing leaves the device.
