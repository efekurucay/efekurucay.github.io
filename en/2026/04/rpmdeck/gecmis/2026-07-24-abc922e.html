rpmdeck

April 2026 · project · JavaScript · source · history

A terminal music player with a spinning ASCII vinyl record in the corner. Zero npm dependencies, powered by mpv.

Why

Half toy, half player. The record spins while the track plays — a small thing that makes a terminal feel less like a terminal. It carries over from the years I spent making music before software.

What it does

  • Plays local audio through mpv
  • Now-playing panel with a rotating ASCII record and a progress bar
  • Zero dependencies — no npm tree to rot
