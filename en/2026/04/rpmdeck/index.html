rpmdeck

April 2026 · project · JavaScript · source · history

A terminal music player with a spinning ASCII vinyl record and a progress bar in the corner. Plays local audio through mpv, with zero npm dependencies.

Half toy, half player. The record spins while the track plays — a small thing that makes a terminal feel less like a terminal. It carries over from the years I spent making music before software.
