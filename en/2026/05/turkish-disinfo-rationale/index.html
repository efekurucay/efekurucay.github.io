Turkish disinformation rationale generator

8 May 2026 · project · Python · source · history

Given a claim in Turkish, this LoRA writes out the reasoning for why the claim holds or fails. Supervised fine-tuning on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B with Tinker.

Why

In fact-checking, the label is not the valuable part — the reasoning is. Saying "false" convinces nobody. Showing which data, which source and which step makes it false does.

There is also the Turkish side of it. Large models perform noticeably worse in Turkish than in English, and the gap widens on tasks like disinformation that depend on context and tone.
