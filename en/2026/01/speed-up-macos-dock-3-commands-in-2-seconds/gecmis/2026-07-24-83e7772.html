Speed Up macOS Dock: 3 Commands in 2 Seconds

15 January 2026 · writing · history

Ever noticed how macOS Dock feels painfully slow when auto-hiding? You move your cursor to the bottom, and the Dock takes forever to appear. Apple doesn’t let you change this animation speed in System Settings.

Solution: Terminal

Open Terminal and paste these commands:

defaults write com.apple.dock autohide-delay -int 0
defaults write com.apple.dock autohide-time-modifier -float 0.45
killall Dock

Done. Your Dock now appears instantly.

What Did We Do?

  • autohide-delay -int 0: Removed the delay when your cursor touches the screen bottom​
  • autohide-time-modifier -float 0.45: Set animation duration to 0.45 seconds​
  • killall Dock: Restarted Dock to apply changes​

You can adjust 0.45 to your preference. Want faster? Try 0.2. Want slower? Try 0.8.

To Restore Defaults

To Restore Defaults

defaults delete com.apple.dock autohide-delay
defaults delete com.apple.dock autohide-time-modifier
killall Dock
