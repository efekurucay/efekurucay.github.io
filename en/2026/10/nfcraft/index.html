NFCraft

October 2026 · project · Kotlin, Swift · source · history

A modern toolkit to read, write, automate and emulate NFC tags. One monorepo, two native apps: a full-featured Android app (Kotlin · Jetpack Compose) and an iOS app that covers the subset iOS permits (SwiftUI · CoreNFC).

Why

Existing NFC apps are dated and cluttered. I built NFCraft from the ground up — clean and open source, on top of open NFC Forum standards and public chip datasheets. No ads, no analytics, no trackers; the only permission the core needs is NFC.

What it does

  • Read — chip identification (NTAG, Ultralight, MIFARE Classic/Plus, DESFire, ICODE, FeliCa), UID, manufacturer originality-signature verification (pure-Kotlin secp128r1 ECDSA), and NDEF decoding
  • Write — 11 NDEF record types (URL, text, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, contact, email, SMS, geo, app, MIME, phone), live size, lock-after-write
  • Tasks — 220 automation tasks across 10 categories, run on scan with if/else/end conditions. Root/dangerous tasks are deny-by-default: a Settings opt-in, an allow-list and a per-task confirmation
  • Emulate — turn the phone into an NFC Forum Type 4 tag (Host Card Emulation)
  • Tools — memory dump, password protection, make-read-only; profiles (JSON/CSV), a raw APDU console, a home-screen widget and launcher shortcuts

iOS

iOS exposes only a small slice of the NFC stack to third-party apps — an Apple limitation. The iOS app covers NDEF read/write and tag identity; raw APDU, MIFARE Classic, emulation (HCE) and the task engine are not available on iOS. NFC also requires a paid Apple Developer account — a free-signed sideload cannot use NFC at all.
