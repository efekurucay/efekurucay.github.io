BİLMÖK

BİLMÖK (Bilgisayar Mühendisliği Öğrencileri Kongresi) is Turkey's largest computer engineering student congress. I was vice-president of the organizing committee for the 21st — 22–23 February 2025, hosted by Akdeniz University in Antalya. I emceed the two-day event and helped coordinate the organizing team through talks spanning AI, cybersecurity, software development and tech ethics.
